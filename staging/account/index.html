Private league

ESPN shows a private league only to its members. Until the DDF browser extension is ready, add your ESPN session keys once, from a computer. They're stored encrypted and you can disconnect anytime.

How to copy your ESPN session keys
  1. On a computer, sign in at fantasy.espn.com and open your league.
  2. Open the browser's developer tools (Chrome: View › Developer › Developer Tools).
  3. Go to Application › Cookies › https://fantasy.espn.com.
  4. Copy the value of espn_s2 and of SWID into the boxes below.
