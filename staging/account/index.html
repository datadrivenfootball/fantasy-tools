Your ESPN league

Paste your league link, or type your league ID. For most leagues that's all it takes.

Where do I find my league ID?
espn.com/…/league?leagueId=123456
  1. Open fantasy.espn.com in your browser and open your league.
  2. Your league ID is the number after leagueId= in the address.
  3. Copy the whole address and paste it here. DDF finds the number for you.

Add the DDF helper to Chrome
Add the DDF helper (early test build)
  1. Download the DDF helper and open the zip. You get a folder named ddf-espn-extension.
  2. In Chrome, open the menu, then Extensions, then Manage Extensions.
  3. Switch on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and choose that folder.
  4. Sign in at espn.com in Chrome, then come back to this tab.

No sign-in neededAsk your league manager

One switch in ESPN's league settings lets DDF read your league. It only lets people look. Nobody can change anything.

Make League Viewable to PublicYes
I'm the league manager
  1. Open your league at fantasy.espn.com.
  2. Open LM Tools, then Basic Settings, then Edit.
  3. Set Make League Viewable to Public to Yes and save.

Or finish on a computer

On a computer, signing in at ESPN takes two clicks. Send yourself this page and open it there.

None of these work for me

A last resort for phones: save a special bookmark once, then tap it on espn.com.

  1. Save the DDF ESPN bookmark.

    iPhone: tap Share, then Add Bookmark, and save it as DDF ESPN. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap DDF ESPN, clear the address, paste, and tap Done.

    Android: tap the star to bookmark this page, tap Edit, name it DDF ESPN, and paste over the address.

  2. Open ESPN in this tab and sign in.
  3. Tap the DDF ESPN bookmark while you're on espn.com. On Android, type DDF ESPN in the address bar and tap the bookmark. You come back here with your leagues listed.
