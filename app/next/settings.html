Position counts

A league that must start two QBs sets QB to 2 and SUPERFLEX to 0.
ⓘSUPERFLEX is an extra starting spot that can be filled by a QB, RB, WR or TE. Most teams put a second quarterback there because QBs score the most, so quarterbacks become worth more. Sites call it Superflex, SUPER_FLEX, SFLX, OP, Q/W/R/T or QWRT. Most leagues have 0 or 1. It is not the same as a 2-QB league, which requires you to start two quarterbacks.

Kickers and defenses

We do not value kickers or defenses, but they fill starting spots on every roster, so we count them in your roster spots, open spots and drops. They do not change player values today.
