1 · The pipeline

One recipe for all seven sources

Three projections (ESPN, CBS rest of season, Razzball), three published charts (USA Today, FantasyPros, CBS) and one observed-trade estimate (FantasyCalc, estimated from completed fantasy trades) all go through the same steps. No source is an anchor. DDF Value is the mean of the four trade charts' Adjusted values (the three published charts and FantasyCalc) and the projections are the alternate version; Indexed exists only to show the charts on one scale and never feeds DDF Value.

Sources publish a new week at different times, so every source carries its own week. While some are on week N and others on N+1, the values are priced one week at a time: the N+1 view uses only the sources that have published N+1, and the week N view uses only week N data (a source that already moved on is read from its saved week N). A source still on N is left out of the N+1 view and named, never mixed in. The page opens on N+1 once more than half of the sources feeding DDF Value at your league setting have published it; at exactly half it stays on N.

    2 · Checks

    Every source spends the same pie

    Each source's Adjusted values and its value above waivers values sum to the league pie over its work list (its listed players plus, for a chart, its estimated ones), and each of its eight group totals equals its budget. These are the deploy gate.

    Indexed: one factor per chart, set against DDF Value

    The factor makes the chart's total over the players it lists (and that have a DDF Value) equal DDF Value's total over the same players. One positive factor keeps the publisher's order exactly; the rank guard counts pairs the chart ranks apart that Indexed puts the wrong way round.

    Where each position's curve starts

    The top value at each position. Nothing pins these: each source starts where its own values put it after the pie and the weights.

    3 · Curves

    Value by positional rank

    Each line is one series sorted by its own values, so you see its shape, not its player order. Dashed lines mark the league's starter and rostered counts at this position (the allocation in step 2).

    4 · Weights

    How each source would split the pie, and the average that does

    Each source's own split of its value above waivers into eight groups (position × starter/bench). With expected starts, a group is the sum of its start-worthy or fill-in parts and the bench share comes out of them; otherwise bench is normalized to the set bench share. DDF weights are the average of the sources' mixes. Every source's Adjusted values are paid out on the DDF weights, so these rows show how far each source's own view is moved. Cells are tinted by the gap to the DDF weight: red where the source gives more, blue where less.

    8 · League settings

    What moves when the league changes

    The pie, the DDF weights and the top value per position at other team counts and scorings (standard roster). The charts' lists are saved at 12 teams; at every other team count the same lists are deconstructed for that league.
