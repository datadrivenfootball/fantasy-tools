What we learned. A player's raw history overstates his future reception effect by 1.4 to 5 times; shrinking it is the part of your logic the data support outright. The two publishers each apply a stable scoring response of their own (0.82 to 0.84 agreement with themselves week to week) that the other does not share (0.29 between them), so no single within-position target exists at WR and TE. On position levels they agree with each other and with DDF's own pricing: a point is worth about 0.7 as much chart value at Full for WR and TE. v4 held that rate fixed, which is why it overshot levels; letting it fall as DDF does removes the overshoot but then lands short.
